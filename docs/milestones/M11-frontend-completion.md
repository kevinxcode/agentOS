==================================================
UI/UX QUALITY
==================================================

The product should feel modern, professional, and information-dense without becoming cluttered.

Keep a coherent visual system.

Prefer:

clear hierarchy
clean spacing
responsive layouts
consistent cards
useful empty states
skeleton states
loading indicators
error states
confirmation for destructive actions
keyboard-friendly controls where useful
clear model/provider identification
execution status indicators

Do not redesign working pages merely for aesthetics unless needed for consistency or usability.
